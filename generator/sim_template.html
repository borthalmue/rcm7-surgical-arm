RCM-7 Simulator

129 CAD parts · 7 driven joints · units mm

Drive the arm the way a controller would. Every joint has a motor with a speed and acceleration limit, so commands are followed, not teleported. Every internal part moves at its real ratio: the gear rotors, the ball screw, the pulleys and both redundant belts, and the counterbalance spring. The remote center R stays fixed through all of it.
