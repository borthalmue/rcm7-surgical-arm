RCM-7 Motion Lab

Planner · servo · test bench · mm and rad · joint simulator ↗

Give the arm a start A and a goal B for the tool tip, either as X, Y, Z in millimetres or as joint values in radians. The planner shapes a path, times it against every joint's speed and acceleration limit, runs it through a simulated 1 kHz servo loop on the CAD model's real masses, and grades the result. Teach it a move by jogging the tip and it will reuse that move's shape between any two points.

    How the planner works
    
    
      
      
    1. Read the task
      A and B are tool-tip positions in mm from R. Joint entries (yaw and pitch in rad, insertion in mm) go through forward kinematics first, so both modes mean the same thing.
      2. 
      
    2. Check reach
      Closed-form inverse kinematics about R, refined by Newton steps when the wrist is bent, then checked against every joint range and the cannula interlock (wrist straight below 55 mm insertion).
      3. 
      
    3. Shape the path
      Straight line in space, or a straight line in joint space. Avoidance optimises a cubic Bézier with Nelder–Mead, penalising path length, any part of the shaft entering a keep-out sphere, and leaving a joint range. Learned paths replay a movement primitive fitted to your demonstration.
      4. 
      
    4. Time it
      Fastest uses time-optimal path parameterisation: a backward pass finds the highest speed that can still stop in time, a forward pass rides that limit. Smooth uses a minimum-jerk profile with the shortest duration that stays inside every limit.
      5. 
      
    5. Compute loads
      A mass matrix and gravity vector built from the mass, centre of mass and inertia tensor of each of the 129 CAD parts, plus the reflected motor rotor inertia through each gear ratio and the counterbalance spring.
      6. 
      
    6. Run the servo
      A 1 kHz computed-torque PID with feedforward drives a plant that is 8–12 % heavier than the controller assumes, with friction and 20-bit encoder steps. Ideal mode removes all three.
      7. 
      
    7. Grade
      Pass needs a final error under 50 µm, every joint within its speed and acceleration limit, drive torque under the gearbox peak, the shaft clear of every zone and the remote center unmoved. RMS load above a motor's continuous rating is a warning.
      8.
